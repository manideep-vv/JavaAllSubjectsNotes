🪑 10-minute setup (do this once)
1) Seat height (foundation)
Raise/lower so feet are flat, heels touching the floor
Knees slightly lower than hips (a tiny downward slope)

👉 If your desk is now high → raise the chair and use a footrest (even a box)

2) Lumbar support (biggest fix)

Jupiter’s lumbar is weak, so add your own:

Roll a small towel (fist-size) or use a thin pillow
Place it at the belt line (L4–L5 area)

👉 You should feel your lower back gently pushed forward—not forced

3) Seat depth (hidden issue)
Sit all the way back
Leave 2–3 fingers gap between seat edge and back of knees

👉 If the seat feels long → sit slightly forward + keep lumbar pillow in place

4) Backrest angle (don’t sit 90°)
Recline a bit to 100–110°
Let the backrest carry some weight

👉 Straight 90° = more fatigue over time

5) Armrests (reduce shoulder strain)
Height: elbows at ~90°
Keep shoulders relaxed (not raised)
If armrests are too hard/high → lower them or don’t rely on them constantly
⏱️ During the day (this matters more than you think)
6) Micro-break rule
Every 45–60 mins → stand for 2–3 mins
Walk, stretch, or just stand
7) Quick reset (30 seconds)

When you feel discomfort:

Sit back fully
Re-position lumbar towel
Take 3 deep breaths
Slight recline again


8) Laptop/monitor height
Top of screen at eye level
If low → stack books under laptop

👉 This alone reduces back rounding

🧠 What to expect

After these changes:

You should be able to push from 6 hrs → ~7–8 hrs more comfortably
Still not perfect, but much better for this week


⚠️ What NOT to do
Don’t sit forward for long (kills your back)
Don’t remove lumbar support after adding it
Don’t sit continuously for 2–3 hrs without break
